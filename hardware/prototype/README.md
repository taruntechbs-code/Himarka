# Physical Prototype Enclosure & Assembly

This folder documents the physical fabrication, insulation properties, and assembly layout of the SITAGARAA cold storage chamber.

## Enclosure Structure

1. **Insulation Chamber**:
   - High-density Expanded Polystyrene (EPS) / Polyurethane Foam (PUF) thermal insulation lining.
   - Sealed lid with rubber airtight gasket to retain cooling efficiency.
   
2. **Electronics Enclosure**:
   - External moisture-resistant junction box housing the ESP32-CAM board, 5V buck converter, and relay module.
   - Cable pass-through gland separating moist interior cold zone from high-reliability electronic control circuits.

3. **Sensor Placement**:
   - **DHT22**: Mounted mid-level on interior side wall, shielded from direct cold-plate condensation.
   - **MQ-135**: Mounted upper-rear inside chamber where rising organic volatile gases collect.
   - **OV2640 Camera Lens**: Mounted top-center ceiling angling downward at 45 degrees towards the produce tray.
